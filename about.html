About Me

Creating content that connects, tells stories, and turns audiences into communities and clients.

I'm Julie Manhaudier, a content creator and communications professional from France, based in Málaga. My background across media, cultural institutions and a film festival has shaped the way I create: with vision, storytelling, and attention to what makes people stop, feel, and act.

I'm an ambitious and proactive person who enjoys creating through image, video, and story, from concept to final cut. Self-taught in video editing, I'm driven by curiosity, initiative, and the challenge of turning ideas into projects that make an impact. That same mindset shapes the way I approach life, with a deep appreciation for sport, music, and the people I care about most.

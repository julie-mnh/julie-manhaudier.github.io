The concept

For this campaign, the Alianza Francesa de Málaga moved away from a classic sponsored post and promoted a video instead: a face-camera piece I created and hosted, presenting the school's summer courses. We boosted it with a paid budget, and the response went far beyond what we expected.

The process

Once the ad started generating inbound messages, I took ownership of the full lead funnel: qualifying each person through conversation, guiding them to a placement test with one of our teachers, and tracking everything in a database I built from scratch. I worked closely with the director, the pedagogical coordinator and the secretary to align on strategy and follow up on leads.

38K+ Views
150+ Qualified leads
10+ Enrolments generated

What I learned

This was my first real paid ad campaign, and my first time managing an entire acquisition process solo, from the ad to the conversation to the follow-up. I learned to treat every lead individually: asking the right questions to understand their level and needs, rather than sending the same generic reply to everyone. It also showed me how content and conversion work together, and made me want to go further in that direction.

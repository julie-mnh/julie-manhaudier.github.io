Rendez-vous

Video production On-camera hosting Social media content

The concept

Rendez-vous is an interview video series I created and produced for the Alianza Francesa de Málaga. The idea: promote the school through storytelling rather than classic ads, giving a voice to students, alumni and figures connected to French culture.

The process

I built the format from scratch and produced each episode end to end: research, pre interview, filming, hosting, editing and publishing. Working alone meant working smarter: instead of editing full interviews, I cut the strongest moments into short videos made for social media.

What I learned

Rendez-vous was my first time hosting on camera, in Spanish, my third language. I learned that a good interview is not a list of questions but a conversation: writing questions that bring real answers, making guests feel at ease, and staying curious enough to follow where they go.
